# The Table — Player Handbook (v14)

This is the concise player reference for the canonical v14 rules. Your private contract and the controls show the exact choices available to you.

## Goal and setup

The Table is for **3–4 players**; four is the full experience. Complete your secret Objective, survive until leaving becomes available, and leave before the others. Your Role and Objective are separate.

Starting kits are:

| Role | Starting kit | Coins |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Enforcer | Pistol, Bullet, Cloth | 2 |
| Medic | Medicine, Antidote, Knife | 3 |
| Smuggler | Cloth, Bag, Poison | 4 |
| Locksmith | Key, Lockpick, Knife | 3 |
| Broker | Spyglass, Handcuffs, Knife | 5 |
| Scavenger | Knife, Medicine, Crowbar | 3 |

Exactly one random player privately receives one bonus item from Knife, Poison, Spyglass, Handcuffs, Medicine, Key, Cloth, or Bullet. Before Round 1, everyone locks one eligible starting item in their default Pocket. Starting visible inventories reveal together after all choices are locked. The Standard Chest is present in Round 1. There is no pre-round shop.

## Turns, actions, and abilities

Seat order is fixed. A normal turn lasts 60 seconds and allows one Primary Action plus any legal Free Actions. Primary Actions include using an item, Trade, and Threat. If the timer expires, the Primary Action is skipped.

Global voice, global/private text, and reviewing information are Free Actions. Role ability costs are:

- **Quick Draw:** modifier inside a legal Pistol action, once per match; it is not a second action.
- **Field Dressing:** passive when the Medic revives another player.
- **Private Stock:** passive.
- **Tumbler Sense:** **Free Action, once per complete Round**. It privately reports whether the Standard Chest contains an Artifact and whether it contains a Rare regular item, never exact contents.
- **Margin:** passive.
- **Salvage:** passive trigger, once per Intermission.

## Trade and Threat

Trade and Threat consume the actor's Primary Action and open a separate 30-second response while the acting timer pauses.

- A Trade transfers assets only after an explicit **Accept**. **No response → Decline.** Nothing transfers and locks release.
- A Threat may be **Complied** with or **Refused**. **No response → Comply.** Covered items are always revealed after resolution.
- Pocket items cannot be offered, requested, or demanded.
- Downed or disconnected players cannot initiate or be targeted by Trade or Threat.

## Inventory and combat

You have 5 maximum HP, six visible inventory slots, and one default hidden Pocket. A visible Bag grants a second Pocket. Other players see Pocket occupancy, not contents. Pocket items stay locked until used, stolen, destroyed, or otherwise removed. A full visible inventory starts a 15-second discard-or-replace choice.

- Knife: one-use, adjacent only, 2 damage.
- Pistol: reusable, any non-Downed player, 3 damage; spends one Bullet unless Quick Draw is used.
- Poison: 1 damage at the start of the next three living turns; pauses while Downed.
- Armor: blocks one complete Knife or Pistol hit, then is consumed.
- Medicine: heals 2 HP or revives to 2 HP; a Medic revives another player to 3 HP.

## Downed and rescue

At 0 HP you become **Downed**, not immediately dead. The aggressor may immediately loot one visible item. Every other living player, on their normal turn before the aggressor returns, may take one optional Downed Interaction in addition to their Primary Action: loot one visible item or use an appropriate item to help. On your own Downed turn, you may self-revive with Medicine, including Pocketed Medicine; otherwise you pass and remain Downed.

If you are still Downed, you permanently die **immediately before the original aggressor would receive their next normal turn**. Intermission can occur inside this rescue window.

While Downed you cannot take an ordinary Primary Action or shop, but you remain socially present. You may use global voice and global/private text, read Chat and the public event log, inspect public table/player information, your own Objective and inventory/Pockets, read the handbook, and follow turn order and the rescue deadline.

## Objectives, failure, and leaving

Objective states are **ACTIVE**, **EXIT READY**, **BANKED**, and **FAILED**. Progress and failure reasons on your contract come from the authoritative table state.

When you complete your Objective, **Leave Table unlocks at the start of your next normal turn**. You must survive the intervening table state. A sustained Objective locks again if its requirement is lost before exit; its first readiness reward does not repeat. First normal readiness grants 3 Coins; contingency readiness grants 2 Coins.

An Objective becomes FAILED only when it is permanently impossible, never merely difficult or temporarily unavailable. Your private contract explains the exact reason. On your own normal turn after failure, you may keep playing or **Concede** for 0 points. Concede is final, makes you a read-only Spectator, and does not reveal the failed Objective to living players.

## Pipelined Intermission

Every Intermission refreshes the four-slot Public Shop and each living, non-Downed player's private Black Market. A brief die/coin reveal establishes the visible Public order.

1. **Preparation — 15 seconds:** everyone may inspect Public stock, sell old eligible visible items, fill empty Pockets, use Free Actions, and plan. Private markets remain locked.
2. **Public decisions — 15 seconds each:** in order, each eligible player buys at most one Public item or Passes. Timeout means Pass.
3. **Private markets in parallel:** resolving your Public decision immediately unlocks your private market while the next player takes their Public turn. You may buy at most one private item and finish when ready.
4. **Final grace — 20 seconds:** after the last Public decision, unfinished players complete private shopping and Pocket management. Intermission ends early if everyone finishes; otherwise timeout passes unfinished private choices.

You may sell any number of **old** eligible visible items at any point before finishing, including after buying. An item acquired during the current Intermission **cannot be sold until the next Intermission**. Artifacts and the Passage Token cannot be sold. New purchases may fill empty Pockets; existing Pocket contents cannot be voluntarily swapped.

## Chests, exits, and spectators

The opening Standard Chest uses the weaker Round-1 loot table and contains no Rare item, Artifact, Black Key, or Quest item. It opens with Key, Lockpick, or Crowbar. The Black Chest appears in Round 3 and requires a Black Key. Opening a Chest is a Primary Action.

Earlier successful exits score more: 3-player scores are 100/65/35; 4-player scores are 100/70/45/25. Contingency scores receive 80%, rounded to the nearest whole number. Ordinary death, unresolved Objectives, and Concede score 0.

Players who permanently die, successfully exit, or Concede become Spectators. Spectators receive god view but cannot communicate or interact.
