# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Three or four players sharing a realtime social game on desktop, and verified spectators following the same match. The approved v14 revision, its Round Income/Wildcards patch, and GAME_RULES.md govern gameplay; supplied standalone HTML and art handoffs govern presentation.

## Product Purpose

Make a complete match playable from joining through objectives, negotiation, survival, scoring, and rematch. The authoritative room projection supplies every visible fact and action capability.

## Brand Personality

Private motives, restrained tension, and a physical table. Preserve the supplied table rendering and existing dark, warm metal visual language.

## Anti-references

Do not ship the prototype runtime or its conflicting gameplay data. Avoid controls that imply hidden knowledge or speculative action legality.

## Design Principles

- Keep the current decision and deadline easy to find.
- Separate public facts from explicitly labeled private information.
- Preserve a full-viewport seated table without page scrolling or a persistent dashboard sidebar.
- Keep free context, chat, and private drafts available while Downed or responding to a timed decision.
- Render server state; keep only drafts and view preferences locally.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Primary controls have at least 56px targets. Use semantic forms, visible focus, text status labels, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion support. The game remains playable when the 3D renderer is unavailable.
