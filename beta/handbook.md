# The Table — concise Player Handbook

This is a concise transcription of the player-facing How to Play and Item handbook. Your private contract and the visible game controls give the exact conditions relevant to you.

## Goal, setup, turns

The game supports 3–4 players. Your secret Objective is separate from your Role; a Role gives starting equipment and a small ability. Complete your contract, then leave before the other players. Completing it does not make you safe: Leave Table becomes available on your next normal turn.

Before Round 1, choose one eligible starting item for your default Pocket. One random player privately receives a modest bonus item and may hide it instead. Starting visible inventories are revealed together after everyone locks their choice. Shopping begins after the first complete Round.

Players act in fixed seat order. Each active player receives one turn per Round, followed by Intermission. After each complete Round, every living player still at the table (including Downed players) receives +2 Coins before shopping. Dead, exited, and conceded players receive none. A normal turn lasts 60 seconds. Choose one Primary Action: Use Item (including opening a Chest), Trade, Threaten, or Draw Wildcard (from Round 3, once per match). Talking, messaging, reviewing your Objective, inspecting public information, and reading handbooks are Free Actions. If the turn timer expires, your turn is skipped.

## Trade, Threat, communication

A Trade uses your Primary Action. Offer or request any number of visible items and Coins; offering nothing is legal. Pocket items cannot be traded. A response lasts 30 seconds; no response automatically declines the Trade. Downed players cannot Trade or be a Trade target.

A Threat demands visible items and/or Coins. An open Threat shows a valid weapon. A covered Threat hides a weapon or bluff under Cloth. Comply transfers the demand safely; Refuse risks the attack. No response automatically complies. A Knife threatens adjacent players only; a covered Knife aimed farther away is a bluff. A Pistol reaches anyone but needs a Bullet. Covered items are always revealed when the interaction resolves.

Living players may use global voice, global text, and private text at any time. Promises are not binding: bluffing, lying, bargaining, cooperation, threats, betrayals, and temporary alliances are allowed. All communication with other players must use the real game UI.

## Inventory, Pockets, combat, rescue

Start with 5 HP, 6 visible inventory slots, and 1 hidden Pocket. Everyone sees visible items and whether a Pocket is occupied, but not its contents. Pocketed items stay locked until used, stolen, destroyed, or otherwise removed; they cannot normally be traded or demanded. A Bag gives a second Pocket. If your full inventory receives an item, you have 15 seconds to discard it or replace a visible item.

A Knife deals 2 damage to an adjacent player and is consumed. A Pistol deals 3 damage to any player, is reusable, and spends one Bullet (the Enforcer also has once-per-match Quick Draw). Up to three Bullets share a slot. Armor blocks one complete Knife or Pistol hit and then breaks; it does not block Poison. Poison deals 1 damage at the start of the next three living turns and pauses while Downed. Antidote removes Poison.

At 0 HP you become Downed. The attacker immediately loots one visible item. Before that attacker's next turn, eligible players may use an extra rescue interaction to loot one visible item, revive with Medicine, or remove Poison using Antidote. Medicine can self-revive on your own Downed turn. Otherwise you remain Downed. If the original attacker's turn returns first, you permanently die and become a Spectator. Medicine heals 2 HP or revives to 2 HP; a Medic reviving another player restores 3 HP.

## Intermission and shops

After Round Income, a die picks the first Public shopper and a coin picks seat-order direction. There are 15 seconds of shared preparation, then 15-second Public decisions in order. Your private market opens after your Public decision, while the next player takes theirs. After the last Public decision there is 20 seconds of final grace, ending early if everyone finishes. Sell eligible old visible items, buy at most one Public Shop item and one Black Market item, and fill empty Pockets. Items acquired this Intermission cannot be resold until the next. Public stock is shared and limited. Your Black Market is private, priced one Coin above base. Smugglers see four offers instead of three.

## Wildcards

The center-circle deck appears in Round 3. Each player may draw once per match on their own normal turn while not Downed. Drawing immediately spends the Primary Action. Only you see the card: Use publicly reveals it before any target selection; Burn discards it face-down without an effect or revealing its identity to other active players. Cards cannot be held, traded, stolen, Pocketed, sold, or protected by Safe. Your normal turn timer continues: a private undecided card burns on expiry/disconnect; a publicly used card with unfinished targets expires without an effect.

| Card | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Seize | Take one visible item from another active player. |
| Destroy | Discard one visible item from another active player. |
| Supply Drop | Every active player receives one random Common/Uncommon normal item. |
| Shatter | Every active player, including you, loses one random visible item, if any. |
| Forced Exchange | Atomically swap one of your visible items with one of another player's. |
| Search Warrant | Publicly reveal one occupied Pocket; Safe blocks it and the card stays spent. |
| Windfall | Every active player gains 2 Coins. |
| Redistribution | The poorest active player takes 1 Coin from each other player who has one; tied recipients are chosen randomly. |
| Scramble | Each active player passes a random visible item to the next active seat clockwise, simultaneously. |
| Second Chance | Heal each living non-Downed player by 1 HP, capped at maximum; no revival or Poison removal. |

Targeted cards preserve normal non-Downed targeting. Global effects include living Downed players except Second Chance. Pockets are protected except from Search Warrant. Normal overflow, equipment, Artifact and Passage Token return rules apply. A card without a legal target is spent without effect.

## Chests and useful items

The Standard Chest is available in Round 1. Its first contents are Common or Uncommon items, or 2 Coins; later refills use the full loot table. Open with a one-use Key or Lockpick, or a reusable Crowbar that destroys one random reward. Unopened contents persist; an opened Chest refills next Round. The Black Chest is available in Round 3, needs a one-use Black Key, contains more valuable rewards and may contain the Passage Token, and refreshes two Rounds after opening. Opening a Chest consumes your Primary Action.

Cloth conceals one committed visible item in a Threat and is consumed afterward. Safe protects one assigned Pocket from inspection and theft. Spyglass privately reveals the exact contents of an unprotected Pocket. Pickpocket steals from an unprotected Pocket; otherwise it fails harmlessly. Handcuffs block the target from initiating Trade or Threat on their next normal turn.

Artifacts are reusable and can be traded or stolen. Brass Idol rerolls one personal Black Market option once per Intermission. Glass Eye privately learns whether one unprotected Pocket contains a weapon once per Round. Red Ledger discounts one Public Shop purchase by 1 Coin once per Intermission. Some contracts require artifacts. Passage Token is a quest item for Escapist and cannot be sold.

## Completing, leaving, scoring

First completion grants 3 Coins. Other players see that you completed an Objective, but its contract stays private until you leave. Sustained objectives must remain valid when you exit. You can stay when the door opens, but later death scores zero. Leave Table exits immediately and cannot be stopped.

Tycoon requires at least 15 Coins simultaneously, including when leaving. Falling below 15 removes Exit-Ready; its readiness reward is granted only once.

| Exit order | 3 players | 4 players |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| First | 100 | 100 |
| Second | 65 | 70 |
| Third | 35 | 45 |
| Fourth | — | 25 |

Permanent death or an unresolved Objective scores 0. A completed contingency scores 80% of the applicable exit value, rounded to the nearest whole point. Consult your own contract for its exact condition.

Players who permanently die or successfully leave become Spectators. Spectators can see Objectives, Pocket contents, Chest contents, and Black Markets, but cannot communicate or interact. In this beta, your independent interview uses only information you learned before that spectator view.
